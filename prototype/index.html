How a run comes back from the HAPS

          

            
[OK]
BigDFT finished; the log is back and is read.

            
[FAILED (exit n)]
BigDFT refused the input or stopped. Its own message is shown; the input is at fault, and the dry run should have said so.

            
[TIMEOUT]
The run reached its time limit. Nothing is wrong with the input: split it, or restart from the last geometry.

            
[HAPS …]
The service failed before or after BigDFT. The input is not at fault and no credit is taken.

          

          
These four outcomes are the ones the pack reports. The workshop never turns a service failure into a verdict on your calculation.
